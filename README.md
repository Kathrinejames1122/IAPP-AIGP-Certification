A dedicated, comprehensive study guide and governance reference repository for candidates preparing for the **IAPP Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP) Certification Exam**.

---

## Exam Overview

The IAPP AIGP certification validates professional knowledge of artificial intelligence systems, responsible AI governance, applicable laws and frameworks, and the governance of AI development, deployment, and use.

- **Exam Format**: Computer-based examination featuring multiple-choice and multi-select questions, including case studies; available at testing centers or through remote proctoring.
- **Target Audience**: AI governance professionals, compliance specialists, legal professionals, risk managers, AI developers, and technology leaders.
- **Prerequisites**: No formal prerequisite is stated in the current IAPP AIGP certification materials.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Understanding the Foundations of AI Governance

- Understand artificial intelligence concepts, capabilities, use cases, and the need for AI governance.
- Establish organizational expectations, governance principles, policies, and procedures.
- Understand responsible AI principles, potential impacts, and AI lifecycle governance.

### 2. Understanding How Laws, Standards and Frameworks Apply to AI

- Understand how existing data privacy laws and other applicable legislation affect AI systems.
- Identify the main elements of AI-specific laws and emerging regulatory requirements.
- Understand relevant industry standards, governance frameworks, and AI risk management tools.

### 3. Understanding How to Govern AI Development

- Govern the design, development, and building of AI systems.
- Oversee the collection and use of training and testing data.
- Govern AI system release, monitoring, maintenance, and ongoing lifecycle management.

### 4. Understanding How to Govern AI Deployment and Use

- Evaluate deployment decisions, organizational requirements, and potential AI-related risks.
- Assess AI systems through impact assessments, testing, validation, and risk evaluation.
- Govern AI deployment and use through monitoring, accountability, and appropriate oversight.
